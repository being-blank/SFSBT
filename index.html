EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATIONS

Qualifications for relevant roles.

Depending on the nature and level of the position, relevant qualifications may include:

  • Diploma in Industrial Safety (DIS) or Advanced Diploma in Industrial Safety (ADIS)
  • Mechanical, Electrical, Civil or related engineering qualifications
  • Recognized Fire & Safety certifications
  • Occupational Health & Safety certifications
  • Relevant professional training in emergency response, fire protection, risk assessment and industrial safety

Additional qualifications, practical field experience, statutory-compliance knowledge, auditing capabilities and industry-specific certifications may be an advantage for specialized positions.
